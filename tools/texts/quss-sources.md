# Texts report — أبيات قس بن ساعدة «في الذاهبين الأولين» (`quss.txt`)

The verses that the accounts of قس's sermon at سوق عكاظ put in his mouth. Scripts: `jahili-build/` (`cfg-quss.js`, `dec-quss.js`, `build.js`, `meter.js`). Log: `jahili-build/out/quss-build-log.txt`. Raw pages: `raw/quss/`.

## 1. Sources
- **Base (K), fully vocalized:** ابن كثير «البداية والنهاية» ت التركي (shamela 4445, p. 1409): https://shamela.ws/book/4445/1409 — 5 verses, in the account of وفد عبد القيس (أبو بكر recites them).
- **Z, vocalized:** أحمد بن حنبل «الزهد» (shamela 8494, p. 2160): https://shamela.ws/book/8494/2160 — 4 verses (no v. 3), rhyme with damma.
- **B:** «الحماسة البصرية» (shamela 6901, p. 689), باب الإنابة والزهد: https://shamela.ws/book/6901/689 — 5 verses.
- **H:** «حماسة البحتري» (shamela 148535, p. 191): https://shamela.ws/book/148535/191 — 3 verses.
- **A:** aldiwan.net (الموسوعة الشعرية), https://www.aldiwan.net/poem78981.html — 5 verses.

## 2. Count
**5 verses**: K, B and A have the same five (Z four, H three). No other verse is attached to them in these sources.

## 4. Harakat and division
- K is vocalized at **100%** (112/112); no mark changed or added; no mark of K is contradicted by Z.
- K prints the verses without cutting the shared word; the meter (مجزوء الكامل، الضرب مرفّل مُتَفاعِلاتُن) cuts it, and it is marked «ـ | ـ» as in the other texts: 1 «الْأَوَّلِيـ | ـنَ» and 5 «مَحَاـ | ـلَةَ» (as H and A print them), 4 «إِلَيْـ | ـيَ» (the doubled ي is cut, as «الذُّلْـ | ـلَ» elsewhere in the app).
- **Meter:** 10 of 10 hemistichs pass.
