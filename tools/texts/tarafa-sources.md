# Texts report — معلقة طرفة بن العبد (`tarafa.txt`)

Riwaya and verse order of الزوزني, collated with التبريزي, ابن الأنباري and ديوان طرفة. 103 verses, الطويل, rhyme ـدِ.

## Method
- Every witness was parsed into verses by script (`muallaqat4-build/exZ.js`, `wit.js`). Hemistichs were compared word by word after stripping harakat and tatweel and unifying alef/yaa/taa marbuta (`col.js`, `zdiff.js`).
- Vocalization (`merge.js`): the base keeps every mark it has. A bare letter takes the mark of the witnesses that have the same word (majority; ties: T, then A, M, W1, W2). A witness whose vowels contradict the base inside the same word is not used for that word (another reading); if it differs only in the case ending, its marks are used for the other letters. Shadda is added only when two witnesses have it (or T alone, when no other witness marks that letter).
- Meter: every hemistich is scanned (`meter2.js`, built on `qahtaniyya-build/meter.js`). When a hemistich did not scan, the merger tried the other attested marks for its letters and kept the one that scans (list below). The remaining failures were checked one by one and corrected by hand (`hfixes.json`), each from a witness or as a form that admits nothing else.
- Raw pages: `raw/tarafa/` (one folder per source; the scripts read them as `pages/<shamela book id>/<page>.html`). Logs: `muallaqat4-build/out/tarafa-*.txt`.

## Sources

- **T: المكتبة الشاملة 37488**, التبريزي «شرح القصائد العشر», المنيرية ١٣٥٢هـ. Verses in parentheses, almost fully vocalized (with typing slips: marks on the wrong letter, «فيِ», «عَلَىَّ»). Independent of Z. Pages 54–99: https://shamela.ws/book/37488/54
- **A: المكتبة الشاملة 124335**, ابن الأنباري «شرح القصائد السبع الطوال الجاهليات», تحقيق عبد السلام هارون (دار المعارف). Verses in parentheses, partly vocalized. Independent of Z. Pages 112–229: https://shamela.ws/book/124335/112
- **M: المكتبة الشاملة 36422**, «ديوان طرفة بن العبد» تحقيق مهدي محمد ناصر الدين (دار الكتب العلمية ١٤٢٣هـ), pages 14–25: https://shamela.ws/book/36422/14. Follows the Zawzani riwaya closely but is a separate text (other readings in places: الرفيع، بها، وتوجست…), so it counts as a witness.

## Count and order
- **103 verses** (Z numbers 1–103).
- **Verses 21–22** («كَأَنَّ كِنَاسَيْ ضَالَةٍ يَكْنُفَانِهَا…» and «لَهَا مِرْفَقَانِ أَفْتَلَانِ كَأَنَّمَا…») are missing from the Shamela copy of Z: its page 107 repeats printed page 97 instead of the page that carries them (Z numbering jumps 20 → 23). They are in T, A and M at that place and were **taken from T**. Their vocalization is T's, checked against A and M.
- Z has 13 («جَمالِيّةٍ وَجْنَاءَ…»), 51 («إِذَا رَجَّعَتْ فِي صَوْتِهَا…») and the last two verses in its form, which T and/or A lack or word differently.
- T and A have two verses that Z lacks: after Z 46 «متى تأتني أصبحك كأسًا رويّة | وإن كنت عنها غانيًا فاغن وازدد», and after Z 61 «ذريني أروّي هامتي في حياتها | مخافة شرب في الحياة مصرّد». M has the first. Not added (Z's count is kept).
- T/A swap Z 37–38.
