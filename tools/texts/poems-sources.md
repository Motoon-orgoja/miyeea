# Texts report — تحفة الأطفال / المنظومة البيقونية

Verse numbers below are the standard numbering (1–61, 1–34).
How the comparison was done: each verse was stripped of harakat and tatweel, alef and yaa forms were unified, and the result was compared word by word against every check source (script output, not by eye).
Changes to the source text in both files: verse numbers, footnote marks, editorial parentheses and brackets, and a few colons/commas were removed. No other changes were made to the source vocalization except the corrections listed below.

---

## 1. تحفة الأطفال والغلمان — سليمان الجمزوري (`tuhfa.txt`)

- **Vocalization source:** المكتبة الشاملة, book 9632 «تحفة الأطفال والغلمان في تجويد القرآن» (the edition with الضباع's notes), pages 2–8:
  https://shamela.ws/book/9632/2 … https://shamela.ws/book/9632/8
- **Wording check sources:**
  - Alukah — متن تحفة الأطفال (partly vocalized): https://www.alukah.net/sharia/0/57837/
  - Arabic Wikisource «تحفة الأطفال» (fully vocalized, but has many typos, tatweel and ى in place of ي): https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/تحفة_الأطفال
  - Vocalization tie-breaker: the mtafsir.net thread «انشراح البال بضبط تحفة الأطفال» by أبو إسحاق الحضرمي. It vocalizes most verses verse by verse, based on what the author received from his shuyukh: https://mtafsir.net/threads/انشراح-البال-بضبط-تحفة-الأطفال-دعوة-للمشاركة.34257/
- **Verse count:** 61 ✔ (all three sources give 61).
- **Headings (10):** مقدمة، أحكام النون الساكنة والتنوين، أحكام النون والميم المشددتين، أحكام الميم الساكنة، حكم لام أل ولام الفعل، في المثلين والمتقاربين والمتجانسين، أقسام المد، أحكام المد، أقسام المد اللازم، الخاتمة. Shamela prints «الخاتمة» in square brackets (an editorial heading). I kept it without the brackets, since Alukah and Wikisource also have a closing heading.

### Wording differences between sources
| Verse | Chosen (Shamela) | Other reading | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9, 12, 21 | وَالثَّانِ | والثاني (Alukah, Wikisource) | Only the shortened form fits the رجز meter (مستفعلن). Shamela and mtafsir agree. |
| 11 | إِلاَّ إِذَا **كَانَا** | كان (Alukah) | Dual (the nūn and the following letter). Shamela, Wikisource and mtafsir agree. |
| 59 | بَدَا **لِذِ** النُّهَى | لذي (Wikisource) | Shamela and Alukah both have لذِ. It is pronounced the same either way. |
| 59 | **تَارِيخُهَا** بُشْرَى | تاريخه (Wikisource) | Shamela and Alukah both have تاريخها, which is the common reading. |
| 35, 36 | وَفَرْعِيٌّ / مَا لاَ … وَلا بِدُونِهِ | spacing only (Wikisource) | Not a real difference. |

### Corrections made to the Shamela vocalization (clear typos, each backed by another source)
| Verse | Shamela | Written as | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | الْمِيهِىِّ | الْمِيهِيِّ | Alif maqsura printed in place of yaa. |
| 9 | يَرْمَلُونَ | يَرْمُلُونَ | The well-known vocalization is يَرْمُلُونَ (mtafsir). Wikisource also has a fatha here, but that is a known misprint. |
| heading | أَحْكَامُ َالمِيمِ / أَحْكَامُ َالمَدِّ | أَحْكَامُ المِيمِ / أَحْكَامُ المَدِّ | Stray fatha on the alif. |
| 28 | وَاللاَّمُ الاُولَى … قَمَرِيَّهْ | وَاللاَّمَ الاُولَى … قَمْرِيَّهْ | It is an object of سَمِّ, like the second hemistich (وَاللاَّمَ الاُخْرَى). The meter needs قَمْرِيَّهْ. Wikisource and mtafsir agree. |
| 52 | مَخَفَّفٌ | مُخَفَّفٌ | Passive participle. Verse 49 in the same source has مُخَفَّفٌ. |
| 59 | بَداَ | بَدَا | Fatha was placed on the alif. |

### Vocalization variants kept as in Shamela (noted for reference)
- 15: قَد ضَّمَّنْتُهَا. The shadda on ض marks the assimilation of the dal. Shamela and mtafsir write it this way on purpose.
- 51: وَالمَدُّ وَسْطُهُ. mtafsir has وَسْطَهُ.
- 41: وَوَاوٌ سُكِّنَا. Wikisource has سَكَنَا. Shamela and mtafsir have سُكِّنَا.
- 1: رَحْمَةِ, in the genitive. mtafsir notes that رحمةَ in some printings is an error.
- A few letters have no haraka in the source (for example الْحَمْدُ للَّهِ, وَآلهِ, وَالثَّالثُ). They were left as they are.

## 2. المنظومة البيقونية — عمر بن محمد البيقوني (`bayquniyya.txt`)

- **Vocalization source:** Alukah — «متن المنظومة البيقونية في علم مصطلح الحديث» (fully vocalized and clean):
  https://www.alukah.net/sharia/0/123682/
- **Wording check sources:**
  - Shamela book 8567 «المنظومة البيقونية» (only partly vocalized), pages 1–6: https://shamela.ws/book/8567/1 … /8567/6
  - Shamela text from «الجامع للمتون العلمية» (الشمراني, دار الوطن), through OpenITI (Shamela book 37317, with footnotes on variants): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenITI/1100AH/master/data/1080IbnMuhammadBayquniDimashqi/1080IbnMuhammadBayquniDimashqi.ManzumaBayquniyya/1080IbnMuhammadBayquniDimashqi.ManzumaBayquniyya.ShamAY0037317-ara1
  - Alukah — «تحقيق متن المنظومة البيقونية» (a critical edition from 4 Azhar manuscripts): https://www.alukah.net/sharia/0/40637/ (and its plain copy https://www.alukah.net/sharia/0/118948/)
  - Arabic Wikisource «منظومة البيقوني»: https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/منظومة_البيقوني
- **Verse count:** 34 ✔. All sources give 34, and verse 34 itself says «فوق الثلاثين بأربع».
- **Heading:** the poem has no section headings, so the file has a single «## المنظومة» line at the top.

### Wording differences between sources
| Verse | Chosen | Other reading (source) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | أَقْسَامًا كَثُرْ | أقسام (الجامع/OpenITI) | All other sources have أقساماً. |
| 11 | **أَوْ** بَعْدَ أَنْ | وبعد (الجامع/OpenITI) | All other sources have أو. |
| 19 | يَنْقُلَ **عَمَّنْ** فَوْقَهُ | ممّن (Alukah tahqiq) | All other sources have عمّن, which is the common reading. |
| 20 | لَكِنْ | لاكن (الجامع/OpenITI) | Spelling only. |
| 21 | **يُخَالِفْ** ثِقَةٌ **بِهِ** الْمَلاَ | يخلف (Shamela 8567, a typo). فيه الملا (Alukah tahqiq) | All other sources have يخالف … به. |
| 24 | غُمُوضٍ اوْ **خَفَا** | خَفَى (vocalization source) | Spelling normalized to خفا, as in all the other sources. |
| 29 | **مُتَّفِقُ** الخَطِّ | متقق (Shamela 8567, Wikisource) | A typo in those sources. |
| 34 | **أَقْسَامُهَا** تَمَّتْ بِخَيْرٍ خُتِمَتْ | أبياتها (الجامع/OpenITI). ثُمَّ in place of تمّت (Alukah tahqiq) | Most of my sources have أقسامها (the vocalization source, Shamela 8567, Wikisource and the Alukah tahqiq). Note: the الجامع footnote says *most printed editions* read «أبياتها», and that الأجهوري preferred it because the poem has 34 verses. Both readings are old. Change it if the site wants that reading. «تمّت» was chosen over «ثمّ» because only one source has ثمّ. |

### Corrections made to the vocalization source
| Verse | Source | Written as | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | أُهَيْلِِ | أُهَيْلِ | Double kasra typo. |

### Vocalization variants kept as in the source (for reference)
- 3: يَشُذَّ. One Alukah copy has يَشِذَّ. Both forms are valid.
- 13: مُعَنْعَنٌ. The Alukah tahqiq has مَعَنْعَنٌ.
- 3, 13, 31: يُعَلّْ / يُسَمّْ / كَرَدّْ. The source writes shadda plus sukun to mark a doubled letter at the rhyme. Other sources write يُعَلْ / يُسَمْ / كَرَدْ.
- 21: فَالشَّاذُ with no shadda on the ذ. The الجامع footnote says the meter requires the lighter form.
