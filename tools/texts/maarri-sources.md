## 4. Harakat
- A is fully vocalized (**99.7% → 100%**; the script's 99.8% counts the second lam of «لِلنَّفَادِ», which by convention carries no mark). **Marks supplied: 4** — 18a للهِ → **لِلَّهِ** (printed as a ligature), 63b حَيَوانٌ → **حَيَوَانٌ**.
- Spacing: 59 «وَلَمْ أَرْ | وَ فَلَا رِيَّ» (the و is the end of «أَرْوَ»; A's page glues it to «فلا»).
- No haraka was corrected. The 15 places where a witness prints another mark were checked one by one: all are typos of the witness (e.g. H «المَرِّيخ», «عَهِدِ»; M «تَعَبُ»; G «معٍ»), except 35b below.
- **Meter (الخفيف):** all 64 verses pass the scanner; 52 hemistich by hemistich, 12 as one line because the word is split between the hemistichs off the foot boundary (3, 18, 20, 23, 25, 31, 38, 50, 55, 56, 61, 64). Split words are split where A splits them.

- **Content (nothing removed):**
  - 62–63 «بان أمر الإله واختلف الناس … والذي حارت البريّة فيه / حيوانٌ مستحدثٌ من جماد»: the verse the critics of المعري quote against him (read as doubt about the resurrection; the commentators read it as the creation of man from clay / the raising of the bodies).
  - 1–3 the opening equates the mourner's wail with the singer's tune and the death-announcement with the glad tidings.
  - 33 «واحبواه الأكفان من ورق المصحف» (shrouding in the leaves of the mushaf, a hyperbole).
  - 37–41 the story of سليمان عليه السلام: «فأنحى على رقاب الجياد» and the son entrusted to the wind who was thrown dead on his throne (an Israelite report used by some commentaries on سورة ص).
