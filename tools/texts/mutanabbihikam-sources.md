# Texts report: ميمية المتنبي «على قدر أهل العزم تأتي العزائم» — `mutanabbihikam.txt`

The scripts are in `hikma2-build/` (described in `zuhdabulatahiya-sources.md`). Raw files are in `raw/mutanabbihikam/`.

## 1. Sources
- **Base (A), vocalized:** aldiwan.net, the text of «الموسوعة الشعرية»: https://www.aldiwan.net/poem10524.html
- **Check 1 (Z), critical edition, unvocalized:** «ديوان أبي الطيب المتنبي», ed. عبد الوهاب عزام (لجنة التأليف والترجمة والنشر). Ketabonline 66865, pp. 374–379: https://ketabonline.com/ar/books/66865
- **Check 2 (W), vocalized:** Wikisource, https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/على_قدر_أهل_العزم_تأتي_العزائم

## 2. Count
A, Z and W all have **46 verses**, in the same order. This is the known count of the poem.

## 3. Wording
Rule: the circulated wording, i.e. the majority of A, Z and W.

| v. | Adopted | A reading | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4b, 21a | ما لا | مالا | Spacing; Z + W |
| 9a | وَالْقَنَا **يَقْرَعُ** الْقَنَا | تَقرَعُ | Z + W |
| 21b | مِنَ **الْفُرْسَانِ** | الأَبطالِ | Z + W. A's word repeats 23a |
| 32a | إِذَا **زَلِقَتْ** | زَلِفَت | Z + W. A's reading is a dotting typo |
| 36b | **لِمَا** شَغَلَتْهَا | بِما | Z + W |
| 46b | الْعِدَى | العِدا | Spelling; Z + W |

These readings were kept with the majority against one witness:
- 5b الْمَلَا. W has الفلا.
- 16a كَأَنَّهُمْ سَرَوْا. W has «كأنّما سروا إليك», which does not scan.
- 19b تُفْهِمُ. W has يُفهم.
- 38a لَا عَنْ. Z has «لا من».
- 44 لَيْسَ … فِيهِ … مِنْهُ. Z has «لست … فيك … منك».

Typos in Z were not counted as variants: 10b تمتئم, 35b حملاتث, 46b وتغليقه, and 5a, where Z drops الطير.

## 4. Harakat
- A's convention was completed in the same way as described in `zuhdabulatahiya-sources.md` §4, and the result was checked against W.
- **Coverage:** A **68.6%** → **99.9%**.
- **Corrected:**
  - 4a وَيَطلِبُ → **وَيَطْلُبُ** (= W).
  - 5a سِلاحُهُ → **سِلَاحَهُ**. It is the object of يُفَدِّي (= W).
  - 16a أَتوكَ → **أَتَوْكَ**. A leaves the ت bare (= W).
  - 26a غائِبُ → **غَائِبٌ** (= W).
  - 32a بِبِطونِها → **بِبُطُونِهَا** (= W).
  - 34a اللَيثَ → **اللَّيْثِ**. It is mudaf ilayh (= W).
- **Meter:** 90 of the 92 hemistichs pass the scanner. The other two, 39b «وَلَكِنَّكَ» and 42b «فَلَا أَنَا», fail only because of the scanner's limits: it does not know the hidden alef of لكنّ or the silent final alef of أنا. Scanned by hand, both are regular الطويل.

## 5. To verify
