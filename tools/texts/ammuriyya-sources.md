## 4. Harakat
- A's convention (no sukun, no vowel before a madd letter, no shadda on a sun letter after the article) was completed mechanically, and every mark of the result was compared by script with W and S. **Coverage: 69.8% → 99.9%** (the only unmarked letters are the assimilated lam of «لِلنَّارِ» and «لِلسَّارِحِينَ»).
- **Corrected:**
  - 1a أَنباءً → **إِنْبَاءً** (= W; مصدر أنبأ).
  - 17a اِفتَرَعتَها → **افْتَرَعَتْهَا** («كفُّ حادثةٍ» is the subject; = W, S).
  - 23b سَرَبِ → **سَرِبِ** (دمٌ سَرِبٌ: سائل; = S).
  - 24a وَالخَطِيِّ → **وَالْخَطِّيِّ** (as A writes the word in v. 55).
  - 28a وَالظَلماءِ → **وَالظَّلْمَاءُ** (mubtada of the hal clause; = W, S).
  - 38b رَوحِ → **رُوحِ** (= S).
  - 39b الرَعَبِ → **الرُّعُبِ** (= S).
  - 45b دَلوا → **دَلْوَا** (dual of دَلْو; = W, S).
  - 56a قَرابينُهُ → **قَرَابِينَهُ** (object of أحذى; = S).
- The remaining 34 mark differences with W and S in the log are typos of those two witnesses (e.g. S «لِيْسَتْ», «الإِسَلَامِ», W «مَشْتَقَّةُ») or equivalent endings; none was adopted.
- **Meter (البسيط، الضرب مخبون):** 142 of 142 hemistichs pass the scanner.
