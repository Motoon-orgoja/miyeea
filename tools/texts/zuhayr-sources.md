# Texts report — معلقة زهير بن أبي سلمى (`zuhayr.txt`)

Riwaya and verse order of الزوزني, collated with التبريزي and ابن الأنباري. 62 verses, الطويل, rhyme ـمِ.

## Method
- Every witness was parsed into verses by script (`muallaqat4-build/exZ.js`, `wit.js`). Hemistichs were compared word by word after stripping harakat and tatweel and unifying alef/yaa/taa marbuta (`col.js`, `zdiff.js`).
- Vocalization (`merge.js`): the base keeps every mark it has. A bare letter takes the mark of the witnesses that have the same word (majority; ties: T, then A, M, W1, W2). A witness whose vowels contradict the base inside the same word is not used for that word (another reading); if it differs only in the case ending, its marks are used for the other letters. Shadda is added only when two witnesses have it (or T alone, when no other witness marks that letter).
- Meter: every hemistich is scanned (`meter2.js`, built on `qahtaniyya-build/meter.js`). When a hemistich did not scan, the merger tried the other attested marks for its letters and kept the one that scans (list below). The remaining failures were checked one by one and corrected by hand (`hfixes.json`), each from a witness or as a form that admits nothing else.
- Raw pages: `raw/zuhayr/` (one folder per source; the scripts read them as `pages/<shamela book id>/<page>.html`). Logs: `muallaqat4-build/out/zuhayr-*.txt`.

## Sources
- **Base (Z): المكتبة الشاملة 11253**, الزوزني «شرح المعلقات السبع», دار إحياء التراث العربي ١٤٢٣هـ (the circulated school text, printed page numbers). The verses are numbered and partly vocalized (about half the letters). Pages 143–162: https://shamela.ws/book/11253/143
- **T: المكتبة الشاملة 37488**, التبريزي «شرح القصائد العشر», المنيرية ١٣٥٢هـ. Verses in parentheses, almost fully vocalized (with typing slips: marks on the wrong letter, «فيِ», «عَلَىَّ»). Independent of Z. Pages 100–127: https://shamela.ws/book/37488/100
- **A: المكتبة الشاملة 124335**, ابن الأنباري «شرح القصائد السبع الطوال الجاهليات», تحقيق عبد السلام هارون (دار المعارف). Verses in parentheses, partly vocalized. Independent of Z. Pages 230–286: https://shamela.ws/book/124335/230
- **W1 / W2: ar.wikisource** «معلقة زهير بن أبي سلمى» and «أمن أم أوفى دمنة لم تكلم (معلقة)» (raw wikitext in `raw/<key>/wikisource/`). Used only for vocalization where the word is the same; they are not always complete. W1 has the Zawzani wording and is fully vocalized; it is the only other source for some of the last verses.
- No digital diwan of Zuhayr was found on Shamela; the collation rests on T, A and the two Wikisource texts.

## Count and order
- **62 verses** (Z numbers 1–62).
- The closing wisdom verses that Z has and T/A do not: 54 («وَمَنْ يَجْعَلِ الْمَعْرُوفَ فِي غَيْرِ أَهْلِهِ…»), 59–62 («وَكَائِنْ تَرَى…», «لِسَانُ الفَتَى…», «وَإِنَّ سَفَاهَ الشَّيْخِ…», «سَأَلْنَا فَأَعْطَيْتُمْ…»). W1 has 54 and 59–61. **Verse 62 is in Z only**; its vocalization was completed by hand where it admits nothing else (listed below).
- T and A have verses Z lacks: «سعى ساعيا غيظ بن مرة بعدما | تبزّل ما بين العشيرة بالدم» (after Z 15) and «ومن لا يزل يسترحل الناس نفسه…» (after Z 55). Not added.
- Order differs a good deal in the wisdom section (T/A put Z 46–57 in another order: inversions at Z 10–12, 22, 33, 39, 46, 49, 51–52, 56).
- Z 9 («عَلَوْنَ بِأَنْمَاطٍ…») and 43 («صَحيحَاتِ مالٍ طالِعَاتٍ بِمَخْرِمِ») are worded differently in T/A («وعالين أنماطًا…», «صحيحات ألف بعد ألف مصتّم»).
