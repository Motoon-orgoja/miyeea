## 5. Attribution (brief)
- The diwan is a late compilation. No early collection exists, and the editions (al-Ṭabbāʿ, Khafājī, «الجوهر النفيس») gather pieces from later adab works.
- «شكوت إلى وكيع» is the most widely quoted of the five.
- The other pieces are ascribed to al-Shāfiʿī mainly in later anthologies, and «نعيب زماننا» is also attributed to others.
- Treat the ascription as traditional, not established.
