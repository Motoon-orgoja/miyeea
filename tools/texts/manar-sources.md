## Why the OpenITI/ketabonline text cannot be used
- **It is 0 % vocalized**, and its spelling is that of a rough transcription. For example, «ثلثه»; «لقياس» for «القياس»; «الى»; «اله» for «آله»; «القرأن»; «الحدفيكون»; «حفضه»; «الهوا»; «الراشين».
- **It is incomplete.**
  - It has about 5,800 words on 26 pages, ending at the chapter on الإكراه.
  - After the definition of the Book («…وهو اسم للنظم والمعنى ....») it jumps straight to «باب البيان واقسام السنه». This leaves out the whole section on the divisions of the Book: الخاص والعام، المشترك، المؤول، الظاهر والنص والمفسر والمحكم…, the main part of the matn. The Dar al-Khayr print has these sections right after the opening.
- **There is no independent digital text** to check it against.

## What it would take (decision needed)
1. **Recommended:** transcribe the matn from a vocalized print. The Dar al-Khayr PDF on archive.org is 73 MB (image only), and the Manar is about 12–15 thousand words. Possible ways:
   - Ask the user for a typed or Word copy (for example the دار سعد الدين edition, ed. أحمد عبد الملك السعدي, 2010).
   - Run a proper Arabic OCR with harakat on the PDF, then proofread it.
   - Use a vocalized edition from a shuruh print where the matn is set apart.

   Any of these then needs the usual collation.
2. **Or** drop this matn for now, or pick a shorter Hanafi usul text that has a vocalized digital source.
