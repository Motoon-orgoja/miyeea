# Texts report: نونية أبي الفتح البستي «عنوان الحكم» (d. 400 AH) — `bustiyya.txt`

Method and scripts: the same as `lamiyyatarab-sources.md` (`hikma-build/`, with `out/bustiyya.collation.txt` and `out/bustiyya.build-log.txt`). Raw downloads are in `raw/bustiyya/`.

## 1. Sources
- **Base (S):** «قصيدة عنوان الحكم», ed. ʿAbd al-Fattāḥ Abū Ghudda (Maktab al-Maṭbūʿāt al-Islāmiyya, Aleppo, 1st ed. 1404/1984), the Shamela text with pages matching the print. Ketabonline 5412: https://ketabonline.com/ar/books/5412. It has 63 numbered verses and the light Shamela vocalization (30% of letters).
- **Check 1 (G):** «التعليق على قصيدة عنوان الحكم», ketabonline 102205. This is the same Abū Ghudda edition with his notes, typed separately and vocalized by hand (39%). It lacks the last verse.
- **Check 2 (W):** Wikisource «عنوان الحكم» (63 verses). It is the most vocalized (49%), but many marks are misplaced (غِلامتهُ، البدَّوِّ، أنصْتَ، نظِر، تُبْذي، إنعامُ، يَدْيكَ…), so it was **not** used to fill harakat. It was used only for the specific readings in §4.

**No fully vocalized digital text was found.** The text is **63.1% vocalized**: S plus the marks of G, filled only where G has the same word and does not contradict S.

## 2. Count
- **63 verses** (S = W = 63; G = 62, without «ما ضرَّ حسّانَها»). Some websites give 64 or more.

## 3. Wording (rule: the circulated wording; S and G are the same edition, so W breaks ties)
| v. | Adopted | S reading | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | لا ثبات | لاثبات | spacing |
| 19 | نَحْو **هَوًى** | هدى | G + W |
| 20 | **بَغْيٌ** وعُدوانُ | بغى | G + W (spelling) |
| 31 | **فالْقَهُ** أبدًا | فألقه | G + W (from لَقِيَ) |
| 38 | **لوارِدِهِ** | لَو ارده | G + W |
| 52 | **ظمآنُ** | ظمان | G (spelling) |

Kept as in S: 3 الدَّارِ (W الدهر), 33 وأفنان (W وأغصان), 38 «ما كلُّ» (G «وما كلُّ», which adds a syllable), 41 ركضوا (W ركبوا), 52 بينها (G بينهما), 55 بك (W فيك), 58 إمعانُ (W إنعام), 63 يصغها (W يقلها).

## 4. Harakat corrected (each one breaks the meter or is a plain error; all logged)
- 17, 19 «وَهُوَ» → **وَهْوَ** (as W prints it), and 38 «فَهُوَ» → **فَهْوَ**. S's «وَهُوَ» does not scan.
- 20 «لِأَن» → **لِأَنَّ** (W), and 60, 61 «فَإِن» → **فَإِنَّ** (W). The meter needs the shadda.
- 27 «يغرنك» → **يَغُرَّنْكَ** (W). G's heavy nun «يغرَّنَّكَ» does not scan.
- 30 «غِلَالَتِهِ» → **غِلَالَتَهُ**. It is the object of تهتك (G).
- 31 «عدوا» → **عدُوًّا** (W).
- 35 «مَال» → **مَالٍ** (W). The meter needs the tanwin; G has «مالِ».
- 51 «أَيهَا» → **أَيُّهَا** (W).
- **Meter:** all 126 hemistichs scan as basīṭ (ḍarb فَعْلُن).

## 5. To verify
- 36% of letters stay bare, especially in vv. 42–43 and 63, where neither S nor G vocalizes. A fully vocalized print, such as Abū Ghudda's own PDF or a school edition, is needed to finish the vocalization.
- 63 «أنْ لم **يصغها** قريعُ الشعر حسّانُ» (S): Wikisource has «يقُلْها».
